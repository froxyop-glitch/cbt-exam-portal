Mandatory CBT Lockdown Rules & Anti-Cheating Protocol

1.

Back-Tap & Back Navigation are strictly Locked: You cannot use the browser back button, backtap gesture, Alt+Left shortcut, or backspace. Trying to do so will pop a security alert toast and keep you on the exam screen.

2.

Mandatory Fullscreen: The exam will launch in full screen. Do not press ESC or exit fullscreen. Exiting fullscreen logs a security violation strike.

3.

No Tab Switching or Minimizing: Switching tabs or opening applications is strictly prohibited. You are allowed a maximum of 3 violation warnings before your exam is automatically submitted.

4.

Continuous Countdown: The exam timer runs continuously. If time runs out, answers are auto-saved and submitted.

5.

Navigation Controls: Use the on-screen question palette and next/previous buttons to navigate questions.
